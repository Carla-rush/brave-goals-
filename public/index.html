R — Rewarding

Does this goal feel genuinely rewarding to you?

If yes — describe what will change in your life once you've achieved it. If not — press back and adapt your goal to one that does, then come back to this question!

You are much more likely to follow through on goals that truly mean something to you — opposed to ones that have been imposed upon you. Body autonomy (which is super supportive for a positive body image!) means you get to decide what's worth working towards.
